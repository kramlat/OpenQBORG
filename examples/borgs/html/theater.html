The Starlight Cinema

Welcome to the cinema. The big screen is a web surface declared in the world: a page with an HTML5 <video>, stretched across eight wall tiles, its sound coming from the screen.

The glowing marquee on the left wall is made by the world's script (scripts/theater.js) out of inline HTML5 and a <canvas>. The script and the marquee talk to each other.

Click the screen to pause or play (it's a real page). The portal by the exit leads back to the courtyard.
