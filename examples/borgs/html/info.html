How this courtyard is built

A QBORG world is a grid of tiles with several layers:

  • Floors come from one image of stacked 256×256 tiles.
  • Walls are blocks with a height; brick and hedge use different strips.
  • Objects are sprites: the trees, animated torches, the fountain, and the blue banners painted onto the north wall.
  • Links: the portals are doorways (gtw); standing near a sign shows a page here (gtw2).
  • Sound: the fountain is a looping WAV sound tile, and the whole courtyard is a MIDI music region.

Open examples/borgs/hello.borg in the OpenQBORG Editor to see every layer.
